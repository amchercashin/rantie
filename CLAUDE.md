## Architecture

**Рантье** (repo/deploy name `rantie`, formerly `heroincome`) — local-first PWA for tracking passive income from stocks, bonds, deposits, real estate, and funds. No backend — all data stored in IndexedDB via Dexie. External reads: MOEX ISS API for prices, bond coupons and exchange rates; heroincome-data repo for payouts — dohod.ru and smart-lab stock dividends and Parus fund distributions, scraped there by GitHub Actions every Monday (a run commits only when the data changed) and read by the app as static JSON on every sync. MOEX ISS stopped serving dividends in autumn 2026 (`/securities/{secid}/dividends.json` answers like an unknown path; dividends moved to the subscribers-only `/iss/cci/corp-actions/dividends`), so `fetchDividends` stays only as a fallback that returns null — a stock in neither dohod.ru nor smart-lab, or a fund outside Parus, has no payout source and gets its payments typed in by hand.

**Stack:** React 19 + React Router v7 (SPA) + Dexie (IndexedDB) + Tailwind v4 + Radix Dialog (bottom sheets) + vite-plugin-pwa + Vitest

### Source-aware income model

Every income-related field on `Asset` tracks its origin:
- `paymentPerUnitSource`: `'fact'` (calculated from payment history) or `'manual'` (user override)
- `frequencySource`: `'moex'` or `'manual'`
- `quantitySource`: `'import'` or `'manual'`
- `ExchangeRate.source`: `'moex'` (indicative rates from MOEX ISS, refreshed by `refreshExchangeRates()` at the start of every sync — even with no exchange-traded assets — and after adding a foreign-currency asset) or `'manual'` (typed in Настройки → Курсы валют, never overwritten; «с биржи» resets it). A foreign asset without a rate is flagged `rateMissing` and left out of the totals; `MissingRateNotice` says so instead of showing 0 ₽.

Manual values override calculated ones. Reset buttons revert to the calculated/imported value.

### Income "pockets" (how a payment becomes monthly income)

`calcAssetAnnualIncomePerUnit(asset, spread, history, now)` is the single entry point; monthly income = annual / 12.
- **Exchange securities** (`isExchangeTraded`: Акции/Облигации/Фонды with a ticker/ISIN) — spread `'year'`: every payment is split into 12 monthly pockets, i.e. annual income = sum of payments over the trailing 12 months. History is the security's own history (MOEX/dohod.ru/Parus), so the window is full and irregular dividends are smoothed. Exception — a security that started paying less than a year ago (`annualizeShortHistory`): its empty pockets would read as missing income, so while its payouts keep coming on rhythm it counts as the average payout × `frequencyPerYear` (never below the plain sum; needs two payouts, or one for a bond, whose frequency is the coupon period). With a year of history both formulas agree, so nothing jumps; a history older than a year keeps the plain sum, where a skipped payment is real. A record dated after today (an announced dividend from MOEX, a payment typed in ahead of time) is not income yet: it is listed among the upcoming payments («ожидается») and starts to count on its date — so the hero number always equals today's point of the chart.
- **Self-recorded income** (rent, deposits, other; `frequencyPerYear > 0`) — spread `'period'`: the latest payout fills a pocket for its own period (12 / frequency months), so monthly rent counts in full at once. The pocket empties when the next payout is overdue by more than half a period (max 45 days). Frequency is editable on the asset page.
- **Timeline / forecast** (`incomeTimeline`) — same formula evaluated month by month for the current quantities: 24 months of history, then 12 months of forecast built from forecasts (`isForecast`: dohod.ru, smart-lab), otherwise last year's payments repeated (a young security's missing payouts continue its rhythm at the average payout, basis `average` «средняя выплата»). `projectIncome` gives the dated payments (calendar, upcoming list); without forecasts it sums exactly to the yearly income. A forecast the board of directors has recommended (`PaymentHistory.recommended`, from smart-lab's calendar) has basis `recommended` «рекомендовано»; other forecasts are basis `forecast` «прогноз». No source says whether the AGM approved a dividend (dohod.ru keeps even approved ones as forecast rows without a declared date until the record date), so the UI never writes «утверждено» or «объявлено»; real records already dated in the future (`scheduled`) read «ожидается».

### Multi-source dividend data

Dividend/distribution history uses source priority: `dohod > smartlab > moex` for stocks, `parus > moex` for funds. Sources are never merged row by row or averaged: dates of one payment differ between them (MOEX 2023 dividend: record date 13.05.2024 on dohod.ru, 14.06.2024 on smart-lab), so a merge would count it twice.
- `DataSource` type: `'moex' | 'dohod' | 'smartlab' | 'parus' | 'import' | 'manual'`
- If dohod.ru covers a stock ticker (its file has rows), only dohod records are stored as payments; old moex and smartlab payment records are deleted on sync
- smart-lab (`data/stocks/smartlab/`, statuses paid / canceled / recommended / forecast / expected) is the source of a stock dohod.ru does not list (`smartlabAsMain`: paid → payment; recommended → forecast with `recommended`, a payment once its record date is past; forecast / expected → forecast while ahead; canceled → a zero forecast while ahead). Under dohod.ru it only refines upcoming dividends (`refineForecasts`): a board recommendation marks dohod's forecast of the same dividend (closest record date within 45 days) `recommended` when date and amount agree — they usually do — or replaces it with smart-lab rows (stored as `smartlab`, forecasts only, so never counted as income); a cancellation removes the forecast and adds a zero one. If smart-lab's file fails to load, the marks disappear until the next sync — dohod's forecasts stay
- If neither covers a ticker, moex is used as fallback
- Funds (ЗПИФы): heroincome-data fund distributions when available (Parus funds), MOEX as fallback. Uses `amountBeforeTax` and `recordDate`. heroincome-data reads the Parus fund list from parus.properties, so a newly listed Parus fund appears there without code changes; files are keyed by ISIN, and `aliases` in `funds/index.json` maps a MOEX code that differs from the ISIN (XTRIUMF). `findFundKey(ticker, isin, secid)` tries every code, directly and through the aliases.
- `heroincome-data.ts` fetches from `raw.githubusercontent.com/amchercashin/heroincome-data`; separate caches for the dohod.ru index (`data/stocks/dohod/`), the smart-lab index (`data/stocks/smartlab/`) and the fund index (`data/funds/`); `resetDohodCache()` forgets all three. smart-lab rows in another currency than RUB are skipped
- `isForecast` flag on `PaymentHistory` marks predicted payments (shown on chart, excluded from income calculations)
- **One source per security at a time** (`moex-sync.ts`, "Payment sources"): payment rows of two sources stored side by side would count every payment twice. When a preferred source is merely unreachable (`isDohodIndexLoaded()` / `isSmartlabIndexLoaded()` / `isFundIndexLoaded()` false — offline, GitHub blocked — or one file failed to load) and its rows are already stored, they stay and the next sources are not asked. When it no longer lists the security, the next source takes over: smart-lab replaces dohod.ru rows as soon as it has rows; MOEX deletes the old rows only once it has answered. A security with nothing stored falls back down the chain as before.
- Bonds use moex only (no dohod coverage)
- `syncAssetPayments(id, { replaceManual })` returns an `outcome`: `loaded` / `no-source` (every source answered, none has payouts — the usual case outside dohod.ru / smart-lab / Parus) / `unreachable` (try later); bonds without coupons return none. Manual payments are replaced only when a source actually returned rows — on `no-source` / `unreachable` they stay. A full sync (`keepManual`) never writes dohod.ru or smart-lab rows for the first time next to manual dividends — both would count, and until smart-lab many stocks outside dohod.ru had their payments typed in: it keeps them and warns that ⟳ on the security replaces them. Toast wording comes from `src/lib/payment-sync-message.ts` and says "нет в открытых источниках", never blames MOEX, so it stays true if MOEX comes back.

### NDFL tax

One rate per account (an ИИС with the tax break at 0%, a folder of rent as self-employed at 4%), set in one place — Настройки → НДФЛ (`ndfl-settings.tsx`), a row per account, new accounts appear there live. The Счета tab doesn't edit rates; it shows "НДФЛ 0%" under an account whose rate isn't 13% (`ndflAccountLabel`).
- Stored in the Dexie `settings` table as `ndfl@{accountId}` (key helpers in `src/services/ndfl-rates.ts`), so rates travel with the backup; `deleteAccount` removes the account's rate.
- `getNdflRates()` → accountId → rate, filling every account with `DEFAULT_NDFL_RATE` (13%) until the user picks one — pure calculators treat a missing account as 0%, so the default lives only there. `useNdflRates()`, `updateNdflRate(accountId, rate)`.
- Tax is applied in one place: `calculatePortfolioSnapshot` (`assetTaxShare`) averages `1 - rate/100` over an asset's holdings by quantity into `CalculatedAssetStats.taxMultiplier`; the projection/timeline (`netFactor`) reuse it. Never read rates directly to tax income or pick a label.
- Rates used to be per category (`ndfl-{category}`). `legacyNdflToAccounts` turns them into account rates (an account whose categories all had one non-default rate keeps it; mixed → 13%) — at startup (`migrateNdflToAccounts` in `main.tsx`) and when restoring an old backup. It leaves the old keys (a rollback to the previous version still finds them) and writes the marker `ndfl@migrated` so it runs once.

### Navigation & View Transitions

Bottom tab bar (`src/components/layout/tab-bar.tsx`) + `AppShell` (large serif title that collapses into the glass header on scroll; `back` prop shows a back chevron with a fallback route).
Page navigation uses the View Transitions API. `withViewTransition(cb, direction)` in `src/lib/view-transition.ts` sets `<html data-nav="forward|back|tab">` so CSS can slide forward/back or crossfade between tabs; the tab bar has its own `view-transition-name` and stays still. `TransitionLink` (with optional `direction`) replaces React Router's `Link` for all in-app navigation. Reduced motion disables all of it.
Jumps within a page use `scrollToWithPeek(target)` (`src/lib/smooth-scroll.ts`), not native `behavior: 'smooth'`: an eased rAF scroll of 0.4–0.7 s that leaves 60px of the content above in view (so it reads as the same page), stops on any touch/wheel/key, and jumps instantly with reduced motion. Tapping Доходность / Капитал on Доход uses it to reach «Ближайшие выплаты» (or «Откуда доход» when there are none).

### Tooling details

- **Tailwind v4** — no `tailwind.config` file. All theme config is in `src/index.css` via `@theme inline` + `@tailwindcss/vite` plugin. Class names are collected only from `src/` and `index.html` (`@import "tailwindcss" source(none)` + `@source`): by default Tailwind scans the whole repository and ships utilities for every class name it meets in docs, plans and this file. A class used from a new place outside `src/` needs its own `@source`.
- **Vitest** — config embedded in `vite.config.ts`. Environment: `happy-dom`, globals enabled. Setup file `tests/setup.ts` imports `fake-indexeddb/auto` and `@testing-library/jest-dom/vitest`.
- **TypeScript** — strict mode with `noUnusedLocals` and `noUnusedParameters`.
- **PWA** — `vite-plugin-pwa` with `registerType: 'prompt'`. User gets update prompt on new deploy, not auto-reload. `PwaUpdatePrompt` also asks the service worker to look for a new version every time the app returns to the foreground — an installed app is rarely loaded anew, so without it a deploy could go unnoticed for days.
- **Chunks** — `vite.config.ts` puts React + React Router and Dexie into chunks of their own (`manualChunks`). They change only when the libraries do, so their file names survive a deploy and the service worker does not download them again; the app's own entry chunk is about 30 kB gzip.
- **Sync writes** — `syncAllAssets` writes the prices of all securities with one `bulkUpdate`: every write to `assets` makes the open screens recalculate the portfolio, so one write means one recalculation (the automatic price refresh at startup is exactly this path).
- **Deploy** — GitHub Pages at subpath `/rantie/` (repo renamed from `heroincome` in Oct 2026). CI sets `BASE_URL=/rantie/` env var; `vite.config.ts` reads it for `base`. Workflow in `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (Node 22): `npx vitest run` goes before the build, so a red test stops the deploy — main is production. CI runs in UTC; tests must not depend on the local time zone. The old path `/heroincome/` is served by the user-site repo `amchercashin.github.io`: a redirect page plus a self-unregistering `sw.js` that retires the old service worker. Same origin, so IndexedDB/localStorage carry over.

## Conventions

- **Russian UI text** — all user-facing strings are in Russian; preserve this when modifying UI.
- **Dark mode only** — hardcoded in `<html class="dark">`, no toggle.
- **Path alias** — `@/*` maps to `src/*`.
- **Tests** — in `tests/` mirroring `src/` structure. Use `fake-indexeddb` for DB tests. Pure function tests dominate; a few component tests in `tests/components/`. Pin the clock (`vi.setSystemTime`) in tests with dates relative to "today".
- **Dexie transactions** — anything that touches several tables runs in one Dexie transaction: `deleteHolding` / `deleteAccount` (remove the asset and its payments with the last holding), `applyImportPlan`, `importAllData`. An asset is never deleted on its own — it goes with its last holding.
- **localStorage** — through `readStorage` / `writeStorage` (`src/lib/safe-storage.ts`), which never throw: storage can be blocked in the browser settings, and a preference is not worth a crash. Keys start with `rt-`; the few `hi-` keys (`hi-a2hs-*`, `hi-onboarding-done`) are historical and stay, because renaming them would forget the user's choices.
- **iOS hardening** — `viewport-fit=cover` in index.html, `touch-action: manipulation` on html, `font-size: 16px` on all inputs (prevents Safari auto-zoom, which stays after blur and makes the page look wider than the screen; never give an input/textarea/select a smaller text class or a `--hi-text-heading`/`body`/`caption`/`micro` token — `tests/components/form-control-font-size.test.ts` enforces it), an inline script in `index.html` that adds `maximum-scale=1` to the viewport on iOS/iPadOS only (stops focus zoom even when the page rests below 100% — Safari page zoom or shrink-to-fit; Safari still allows pinch zoom, Android keeps the plain viewport; `tests/index-html-viewport.test.ts`), `overflow-x: clip` on `body` (nothing may widen the page, or iOS shrinks it to fit), safe-area insets for bottom sheets, no automatic focus on touch screens (`isTouchPrimary()` in `src/lib/input-device.ts`, `pointer: coarse`: a sheet must be readable before the keyboard covers half of it, so `TextInput`/`TextArea` drop `autoFocus` there and `BottomSheet` focuses itself instead of a field or ✕; a raw `<input>` may only pass `autoFocus={!isTouchPrimary()}` — `tests/components/touch-autofocus.test.tsx` enforces it). Do not remove these.
- **Navigation** — use `TransitionLink` (not `Link`) for in-app navigation; use `withViewTransition(cb, direction)` for programmatic `navigate()` calls.
- **Feedback** — confirmations via `useFeedback().confirm()`, results via `toast()`; first-use explanations via `<Hint id="…" more="section">`, not overlays; the full story goes into the guide.
- **Docs lookup** — при работе с Dexie, Tailwind v4, React Router v7 и Radix сверяйся с актуальной документацией через context7, а не полагайся на обучающие данные.
